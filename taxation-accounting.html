UAE Tax Basics

Understand the Main Tax Requirements

General reference information. Actual treatment depends on the business, transaction type and applicable UAE regulations.

Value Added Tax (VAT)

The standard UAE VAT rate is 5%. Mandatory registration generally applies when the value of taxable supplies and imports exceeds AED 375,000 over the relevant period, subject to the applicable rules.

Voluntary VAT Registration

Voluntary VAT registration may be available when taxable supplies, imports or relevant taxable expenses meet the AED 187,500 threshold, subject to the applicable conditions.

Corporate Tax

Under the general UAE Corporate Tax regime, the rate is 0% on taxable income up to AED 375,000 and 9% on the portion above AED 375,000, subject to applicable rules and adjustments.
