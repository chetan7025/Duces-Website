Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about VAT, corporate tax and accounting support in the UAE.

What is the mandatory VAT registration threshold in the UAE?

The general mandatory VAT registration threshold is AED 375,000 in taxable supplies and imports, subject to applicable rules.

What is the voluntary VAT registration threshold?

The general voluntary registration threshold is AED 187,500, subject to the applicable conditions.

What is the standard UAE VAT rate?

The standard VAT rate is 5%. Certain supplies may have different VAT treatments.

What is the standard UAE corporate tax rate?

Under the general regime, 0% applies to the first AED 375,000 of taxable income and 9% applies to taxable income exceeding that amount, subject to applicable rules.

Do Free Zone companies pay corporate tax?

Free Zone companies may qualify for 0% corporate tax on qualifying income if the applicable conditions are met. Non-qualifying income may be subject to 9% tax.

Can DUCES help organise bookkeeping records?

DUCES can provide bookkeeping and record-organisation support within its authorised service scope.

Can DUCES provide statutory audit services?

Statutory audit services must be performed by an appropriately licensed audit professional or firm. They are not implied by general bookkeeping or consultancy support.

What documents are needed for accounting support?

Requirements may include your trade licence, company documents, invoices, bank statements and relevant financial records.

How can I discuss my business requirements?

Contact DUCES through the contact page or WhatsApp to discuss your business requirements and the available scope of support.
