Frequently Asked Questions

Does every UAE business need Corporate Tax registration?

Requirements depend on the applicable UAE tax rules and the business's circumstances. A qualified tax professional can assess your position.

Can you help with VAT registration?

We can help coordinate documentation and connect you with an appropriately qualified provider where required.

Can you help organise accounting records?

We can discuss bookkeeping support and coordination options based on your business requirements.

Are tax outcomes guaranteed?

No. Tax treatment and filing obligations depend on applicable laws and the facts of each business.
