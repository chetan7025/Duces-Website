UAE Tax Information

Understand the Main Tax Requirements

General information to help business owners understand the main UAE tax concepts.

Value Added Tax (VAT)

The standard UAE VAT rate is 5%. Mandatory VAT registration generally applies when taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000 over the relevant period.

Voluntary registration may be available when the relevant threshold of AED 187,500 is met, subject to applicable rules.

UAE Corporate Tax

Under the general UAE Corporate Tax regime, the rate is 0% on taxable income up to AED 375,000 and 9% on taxable income above that amount, subject to applicable rules.

Qualifying Free Zone Persons may be subject to a separate framework for qualifying income. Free Zone status alone does not automatically mean all income is taxed at 0%.

Financial Record Keeping

Businesses should maintain appropriate financial records, invoices, supporting documents and transaction information in line with applicable UAE requirements.

Business-Specific Requirements

Tax treatment and filing obligations can differ depending on the legal structure, business activity, turnover, transactions and applicable exemptions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Taxation & Accounting FAQs

Does every UAE business need to register for VAT?

Not every business must register immediately. Mandatory registration generally applies when taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000 over the relevant period. Voluntary registration may be possible subject to the applicable AED 187,500 threshold and rules.

Is Corporate Tax applicable to Free Zone companies?

Free Zone companies are within the UAE Corporate Tax framework. A Qualifying Free Zone Person may benefit from a 0% rate on qualifying income if all relevant conditions are met. Other income may be subject to different treatment.

Can a small business maintain its own accounts?

Businesses may maintain their own records, but they should ensure the records are complete, accurate and maintained according to applicable requirements.

What documents are needed for accounting support?

Common documents include the Trade Licence, bank statements, invoices, receipts, expense records and previous tax filings, where applicable.

Does DUCES guarantee tax registration or approval?

No. DUCES can provide agreed consultancy and documentation support, but registration decisions and regulatory outcomes remain with the relevant UAE authorities.
