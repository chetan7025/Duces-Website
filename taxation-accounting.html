Understanding VAT in the UAE

General VAT registration thresholds and the standard VAT rate.

Mandatory Registration

AED 375,000

Generally, taxable supplies and imports exceeding the mandatory threshold over the relevant period require VAT registration, subject to applicable rules.

Voluntary Registration

AED 187,500

Eligible businesses may apply voluntarily when they meet the applicable taxable supplies or expenses criteria.

Standard VAT Rate

5%

The standard VAT rate applies to taxable supplies unless a specific zero-rating or exemption rule applies.

VAT Calculator

Calculate VAT addition or extract VAT from a VAT-inclusive amount.

This calculator assumes the standard 5% VAT rate. It does not determine whether a transaction is taxable, zero-rated or exempt. Confirm the correct VAT treatment before issuing invoices or submitting returns.

UAE Corporate Tax Framework

Understand the general corporate tax rates and the importance of taxable income.

0% Corporate Tax Rate

0%

Generally applies to the first AED 375,000 of taxable income under the standard corporate tax regime.

9% Corporate Tax Rate

9%

Generally applies to the portion of taxable income exceeding AED 375,000 under the standard regime.

Qualifying Free Zone Persons

Conditional

A 0% rate may apply to qualifying income when all relevant conditions are met. It is not automatic for every Free Zone company.

Corporate Tax Estimator

Estimate standard-regime corporate tax using taxable income.

This is a simplified estimate under the standard corporate tax regime. It does not calculate accounting profit adjustments, reliefs, exemptions, special categories or Qualifying Free Zone Person treatment. Actual taxable income and tax liability must be determined under applicable UAE legislation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is VAT registration mandatory for every UAE company?

No. Mandatory registration generally depends on taxable supplies and imports meeting the applicable threshold. Specific rules and exceptions may apply.

Does every Free Zone company qualify for 0% corporate tax?

No. A Free Zone company must meet the conditions for Qualifying Free Zone Person status and qualifying income treatment. The 0% rate is not automatic.

Can a business outsource bookkeeping?

Businesses may engage external service providers for bookkeeping and accounting support, subject to applicable licensing and professional requirements.

Does DUCES provide statutory audit services?

Statutory audit work must be performed by an appropriately licensed auditor. DUCES can help coordinate with qualified professionals where required.

Can DUCES represent a business before the FTA?

Formal tax-agent representation is subject to applicable registration and appointment requirements. The scope of assistance will be clarified for each engagement.

What is the difference between accounting profit and taxable income?

Taxable income is determined after applying relevant corporate tax adjustments, exemptions, reliefs and applicable rules. It may differ from accounting profit.

Can I use the calculators for an official tax filing?

No. These calculators provide illustrative estimates only. Actual VAT treatment and corporate tax liability require review of the underlying business transactions and applicable law.
