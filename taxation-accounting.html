Understanding VAT in the UAE

General VAT registration thresholds and the standard VAT rate for businesses operating in the UAE.

Mandatory Registration

Mandatory VAT Threshold

AED 375,000

UAE-resident businesses generally need to register when the value of taxable supplies and imports exceeds this threshold over the relevant previous 12-month period, or is expected to exceed it in the next 30 days.

Voluntary Registration

Voluntary VAT Threshold

AED 187,500

Eligible businesses may apply voluntarily when the relevant taxable supplies, imports or taxable expenses meet the applicable voluntary registration criteria.

Standard VAT Rate

Standard VAT Rate

5%

The standard VAT rate applies to standard-rated taxable supplies. Specific transactions may be zero-rated or exempt under the applicable VAT rules.

Important: VAT registration thresholds are not simply based on total sales or bank deposits. The relevant taxable supplies, imports and qualifying expenses must be assessed under UAE VAT rules. Special rules may apply to non-resident businesses.

Understanding UAE Corporate Tax

The standard corporate tax framework and the separate considerations applicable to qualifying Free Zone entities.

Standard Regime

0% Corporate Tax Rate

0%

Generally applies to the first AED 375,000 of taxable income under the standard corporate tax regime.

Standard Regime

9% Corporate Tax Rate

9%

Generally applies to the portion of taxable income exceeding AED 375,000 under the standard regime.

Free Zone Regime

Qualifying Free Zone Person

Conditional 0%

A Qualifying Free Zone Person may benefit from 0% on qualifying income and 9% on non-qualifying taxable income, subject to the applicable conditions.

Do not assume a Free Zone company is automatically tax-free. Qualifying Free Zone Person status depends on meeting the relevant legal conditions. The standard AED 375,000 threshold calculation in the estimator below is not designed for Qualifying Free Zone Person calculations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common UAE taxation and accounting questions.

Is VAT registration mandatory for every UAE company?

No. Mandatory registration generally depends on taxable supplies and imports meeting the applicable threshold. Specific rules and exceptions may apply.

What is the UAE mandatory VAT registration threshold?

For UAE-resident businesses, the mandatory threshold is generally AED 375,000 of taxable supplies and imports over the relevant previous 12 months, or expected in the next 30 days.

What is the voluntary VAT registration threshold?

The voluntary threshold is AED 187,500. Eligibility may be based on taxable supplies, imports or qualifying taxable expenses, subject to applicable conditions.

Does every Free Zone company qualify for 0% corporate tax?

No. A Free Zone company must meet the conditions for Qualifying Free Zone Person status and qualifying income treatment. The 0% rate is not automatic.

Can a business outsource bookkeeping?

Businesses may engage external service providers for bookkeeping and accounting support, subject to applicable licensing and professional requirements.

Does DUCES provide statutory audit services?

Statutory audit work must be performed by an appropriately licensed auditor. DUCES can help coordinate with qualified professionals where required.

Can DUCES act as my registered tax agent?

Formal tax-agent representation is subject to applicable registration and appointment requirements. The scope of assistance will be clarified for each engagement.

What is the difference between accounting profit and taxable income?

Taxable income is determined after applying relevant corporate tax adjustments, exemptions, reliefs and applicable rules. It may differ from accounting profit.

Can I use the calculators for an official tax filing?

No. These calculators provide illustrative estimates only. Actual VAT treatment and corporate tax liability require review of the underlying business transactions and applicable law.
