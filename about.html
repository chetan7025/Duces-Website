Who We Are

One consultancy contact for multiple stages of your business journey.

Starting and managing a business involves more than obtaining a trade licence. Business owners may also need banking assistance, financial support, accounting, taxation, documentation and residency-related services.


DUCES Consultancy Services is structured to help clients understand these requirements and coordinate the appropriate services according to their individual business needs.


Our approach focuses on clear communication, practical guidance, transparent service scope and organised follow-up.

What We Focus On

The principles that guide the way we work with clients.

Understanding Requirements

We begin by understanding the client's business activity, objectives and service requirements.

Clear Communication

We explain the expected process, documents, service scope and applicable charges before proceeding.

Coordinated Support

We help coordinate related services so clients have a clearer point of contact throughout the process.

Responsible Guidance

We communicate limitations and distinguish consultancy assistance from decisions made by banks and government authorities.

Practical Solutions

We discuss service options according to the client's actual requirements rather than assuming one solution fits everyone.

Ongoing Communication

We aim to keep clients informed about requirements, progress and relevant next steps.
