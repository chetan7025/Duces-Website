Your UAE Business Journey, Supported at Every Stage.

DUCES Consultancy Services helps entrepreneurs, business owners and companies understand and coordinate essential business requirements in the United Arab Emirates.

Who We Are

A UAE-focused consultancy for connected business support services.

Starting and managing a business involves more than obtaining a trade licence. Business owners may also need banking assistance, financial guidance, accounting, taxation, documentation and residency-related services.

DUCES is structured to help clients understand these requirements and coordinate appropriate services according to their business needs.

Our focus is on clear communication, practical guidance, transparent service scope and organised follow-up.
