COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

A few common questions about DUCES and our consultancy services.

What services does DUCES provide?

DUCES provides consultancy and coordination support across UAE business setup, business banking, business finance, taxation, accounting, PRO and visa services.

Can DUCES assist with UAE company formation?

DUCES can guide clients through mainland, Free Zone and offshore setup options, including relevant licensing and documentation considerations.

Can DUCES help with business bank accounts?

DUCES provides banking consultancy and documentation preparation assistance. Account opening decisions remain with the relevant bank.

Does DUCES provide tax and accounting support?

DUCES offers support relating to VAT, corporate tax, accounting, bookkeeping and financial reporting. The scope of each engagement depends on the service agreed.

Can I contact DUCES before starting a business?

Yes. You can contact DUCES to discuss your intended business activity, setup options and related requirements.

Does DUCES guarantee bank or finance approval?

No. Banks and financial institutions independently assess applications and make their own decisions.
