OUR WORK PROFILE

Business Consultancy That Connects Every Stage of Your Journey

DUCES Consultancy Services is a UAE-focused business consultancy supporting entrepreneurs, investors, startups and established companies with business establishment and ongoing business requirements.

Our work covers the practical areas that businesses need to consider while starting and managing operations: company formation, banking preparation, business finance, tax compliance, accounting, bookkeeping and government documentation.

We work around the client's requirements, helping organise information, understand applicable procedures and coordinate agreed services with relevant institutions and service providers.

Our approach is built around one objective: making business-related processes easier to understand and better organised.

What services does DUCES provide?

DUCES provides consultancy and coordination support across UAE business setup, business banking, business finance, taxation, accounting, PRO and visa services.

Can DUCES assist with UAE company formation?

DUCES can guide clients through mainland, Free Zone and offshore setup options, including licensing and documentation considerations.

DUCES provides banking consultancy and documentation preparation assistance. Account opening decisions remain with the relevant bank.
