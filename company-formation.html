Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Mainland and Free Zone?

They operate under different licensing frameworks. The right choice depends on your activity, customer base, location and applicable regulations.

Can a foreign national own 100% of a UAE company?

Many activities permit full foreign ownership, subject to the applicable legal structure, activity and regulatory requirements.

Can I open a business bank account after getting a licence?

You can apply, but each bank conducts its own KYC, compliance and risk assessment. A trade licence does not guarantee account approval.

Can I establish a company without a visa?

Some packages may offer licence-only options. Availability and visa eligibility depend on the selected authority and package.

How much does company formation cost?

Costs depend on jurisdiction, activities, visa allocation, facilities, government charges and other requirements. Use the estimator above as a budget planning tool and request an itemised quotation.

How long does company formation take?

Processing times vary by authority, activity, document readiness and external approvals. Timelines are confirmed after reviewing the application.
