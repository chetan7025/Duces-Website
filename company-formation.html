Frequently Asked Questions

Some important points to consider before starting your UAE company.

What is the difference between Mainland and Free Zone?

Mainland and free zone companies operate under different licensing frameworks. The appropriate structure depends on your activities, intended market and applicable rules.

Can a foreign national own a UAE company?

Many activities permit full foreign ownership, but requirements and exceptions depend on the activity, jurisdiction and regulatory framework.

Does a trade licence guarantee a business bank account?

No. Banks conduct their own compliance, KYC and risk assessments. A trade licence does not guarantee account approval.

Can I start a company without taking a visa?

Visa eligibility and package options vary by jurisdiction and licence structure. Confirm the applicable requirements before selecting a package.

What documents are generally required?

Common requirements may include passport copies, shareholder contact details, proposed business activities and supporting documents. Additional requirements vary.

How much does UAE company formation cost?

Costs depend on jurisdiction, activity, licence type, visa allocation, office requirements and additional government or service fees. Request a quotation based on your requirements.
