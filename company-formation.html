Discuss Offshore Options

Important Offshore considerations

  • An offshore company is not automatically authorised to conduct ordinary business inside the UAE.
  • Visa eligibility and local operating rights may be restricted.
  • Banking is subject to independent bank due diligence and approval.
  • Tax, reporting and economic substance obligations must be reviewed for the relevant jurisdiction.
Offshore incorporation should be assessed against your actual business purpose. It is not a substitute for a Mainland or Free Zone operating licence.

Business Setup FAQs

Answers to common questions about establishing a company in the UAE.

Which is better: Mainland or Free Zone?

Neither is universally better. The right choice depends on your activities, customers, operating model, office needs and authority requirements.

Can a Free Zone company do business in the UAE?

It depends on the activity and applicable rules. Certain activities or arrangements may require additional permissions, approvals or a suitable local structure.

Can I open a bank account immediately after company formation?

Company formation does not guarantee bank account approval. Banks conduct their own due diligence and may request business plans, contracts, invoices, source-of-funds information and other documents.

Does every company setup package include a residence visa?

No. Visa eligibility, allocation, costs and processing are subject to the selected authority, package and applicable immigration requirements.

Does DUCES guarantee a licence, visa or bank account?

No. DUCES provides consultancy and coordination support. Final decisions and approvals are made by the relevant authorities, immigration departments and financial institutions.

Can DUCES help after the company is formed?

Yes. You can also discuss business banking assistance, business finance guidance, tax and accounting support, and PRO and visa services.
