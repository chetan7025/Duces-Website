Business Bank Account Requirements

Banks may request different documents depending on the business activity, ownership, jurisdiction and risk assessment.

  • Valid UAE Trade Licence
  • Memorandum of Association (MOA)
  • Shareholder and Director KYC documents
  • Passport and Emirates ID copies, where applicable
  • Office tenancy or Ejari, if applicable
  • Company profile and business activity details
  • Expected turnover and transaction information
  • Source of funds and supporting documents

This is a general checklist, not a universal bank document requirement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about UAE business banking.

Can a newly established UAE company open a business account?

Newly established companies may apply, subject to each bank's eligibility criteria, documentation, compliance checks and internal assessment.

What documents are generally required?

Common documents include a trade licence, MOA, shareholder identification, company profile and supporting business information. Requirements vary.

Which bank is best for my business?

The appropriate bank depends on your activity, transaction needs, company structure, expected turnover, eligibility and the bank's current offerings.

Does DUCES guarantee business bank account approval?

No. DUCES can provide assistance within its authorised scope, but every bank independently evaluates applications and makes its own approval decision.

Can DUCES help with rejected applications?

We can help you understand documentation gaps and prepare relevant information for further discussion. Any new application remains subject to bank policies.

How can I discuss my banking requirements?

Contact DUCES through WhatsApp or phone to discuss your business profile and requirements.
