Frequently Asked Questions

Can DUCES arrange employment visas?

DUCES provides customer-facing coordination and assistance. Regulated applications and processing steps are handled through the relevant authorised channels where required.

Can you assist with investor and partner visas?

We can help explain requirements and coordinate the application process for eligible applicants, subject to the relevant authority's rules.

Can you help with Emirates ID and medical appointments?

Yes. We can help coordinate the required steps and document preparation. Medical examinations and official identity processes take place through the designated authorities and centres.

Do you provide visa approval guarantees?

No. Visa and residency decisions are made by the relevant UAE authorities. Processing times and outcomes depend on their requirements and approvals.

Can you assist with document attestation?

We can review requirements and coordinate document attestation through the relevant authorised channels, depending on the document and issuing country.
