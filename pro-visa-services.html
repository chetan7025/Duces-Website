Common Visa Document Checklist

Important Information Before Applying

Visa and immigration applications are subject to government rules and approvals.

Eligibility, Approvals & Service Scope

Visa eligibility, required documents, fees and processing timelines depend on the applicant's circumstances and the relevant UAE authority.

DUCES provides consultancy, document coordination and administrative assistance within its authorised business activities. Government application approval is not guaranteed.

Government fees, medical charges, insurance costs, deposits and other third-party charges may be separate from DUCES consultancy or coordination fees.

Where a service requires access or authorisation from a government portal or regulated service provider, the process must be completed through the appropriate authorised channel.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about UAE PRO and visa assistance.

What are PRO services in the UAE?

PRO services generally refer to administrative support and coordination for government-related company documentation, immigration procedures and other official processes.

Can you assist with employment visas?

DUCES can assist with documentation guidance and administrative coordination for eligible employment visa processes.

Can you assist with investor and partner visas?

Yes, DUCES can provide documentation and application coordination support for eligible investor and partner visa processes.

Can you help with family visa applications?

DUCES can help organise documents and coordinate administrative steps for eligible family sponsorship applications.

Can you assist with Emirates ID?

DUCES can provide guidance and documentation coordination support for applicable Emirates ID procedures.

Can you assist with medical fitness appointments?

DUCES can help coordinate the administrative steps through the relevant approved channels. Medical examinations are performed by authorised medical facilities.

How long does a UAE visa application take?

Processing times vary by visa type, applicant circumstances, authority workload and document completeness. No fixed timeline can be guaranteed.

Are government fees included in your service fee?

Not necessarily. Government fees and third-party charges should be confirmed separately in the service quotation.

Can you guarantee visa approval?

No. Final decisions are made by the relevant UAE authorities. DUCES can assist with documentation and coordination but cannot guarantee approval.
