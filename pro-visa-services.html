Frequently Asked Questions

Can DUCES arrange employment visas?

DUCES provides customer-facing service management and coordination. Regulated applications and processing steps are handled through the relevant authorised channels where required.

Can you assist with investor and partner visas?

We can help explain requirements and coordinate the application process for eligible applicants, subject to the relevant authority's rules.

Can you help with Emirates ID and medical appointments?

Yes. We can help coordinate the required steps and document preparation. Medical examinations and official identity processes take place through designated authorities and centres.

Can you assist with Free Zone employee visas?

Yes. We can help coordinate requirements according to the relevant Free Zone authority's procedures and applicable permissions.

Do you provide visa approval guarantees?

No. Visa and residency decisions are made by the relevant UAE authorities. Processing times and outcomes depend on their requirements and approvals.

Can you assist with document attestation?

We can review requirements and coordinate document attestation through relevant authorised channels, depending on the document and issuing country.

Can you assist with visa cancellation and renewal?

Yes. We can help organise the required documents and coordinate renewal or cancellation processes according to the relevant authority's procedures.

Are government fees included in your service charges?

Any applicable government, authority, medical, insurance, typing, delivery and third-party charges will be clarified in the quotation according to the selected service.
