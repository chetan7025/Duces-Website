    Common Banking Document Checklist

    Actual requirements vary by bank, company structure and business activity.

    Company Documents

    • Valid trade licence
    • MOA / incorporation documents, where applicable
    • Lease or facility agreement, if required
    • Shareholder and UBO information

    Business Profile

    • Business model and activity explanation
    • Products or services offered
    • Supplier and customer information
    • Expected transaction countries

    Financial Information

    • Expected annual turnover
    • Source of funds and wealth evidence
    • Bank statements, if available
    • Invoices and supporting contracts

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does a UAE trade licence guarantee a business bank account?

    No. Banks independently assess applications based on KYC, compliance, risk, business activity and other criteria.

    Can a newly established company apply?

    Some banks consider newly established businesses. Requirements and acceptance depend on the institution and applicant profile.

    Can a Free Zone company open a UAE business account?

    Free Zone companies may apply to banks that accept their structure and activity. Approval remains subject to the bank's assessment.

    What documents should I prepare?

    Common documents include the trade licence, incorporation documents, shareholder identification, business profile and supporting financial or transaction evidence.

    How long does account opening take?

    Timelines vary according to bank processes, document completeness, compliance checks and additional information requests.

    Can DUCES guarantee approval?

    No. DUCES provides consultancy and application assistance. The bank independently decides whether to approve or decline an application.
