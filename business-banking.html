    Frequently Asked Questions

    Common questions about business banking in the UAE.

    Does a UAE trade licence guarantee a business bank account?

    No. Banks independently assess applications based on KYC, compliance, business activity, risk and other internal criteria. A trade licence alone does not guarantee account opening.

    Can a newly established company apply for a bank account?

    Some banks consider newly established businesses. Requirements and acceptance depend on the institution, company structure and applicant profile.

    Can a Free Zone company open a UAE business account?

    Free Zone companies may apply to banks that accept their structure and activity. Approval remains subject to the bank's independent assessment.

    What documents should I prepare?

    Common documents include the trade licence, incorporation documents, shareholder identification, business profile and supporting financial or transaction evidence.

    How long does business account opening take?

    Timelines vary according to bank processes, document completeness, compliance checks and additional information requests.

    Can DUCES guarantee bank account approval?

    No. DUCES provides consultancy and documentation assistance. The bank independently decides whether to approve or decline an application.

    Does DUCES work with only one bank?

    DUCES provides independent consultancy on banking options. The relevant institution depends on the company profile, requirements and the bank's current policies.

    Can I apply for an account before starting business operations?

    Some institutions consider applications from newly established companies. They may request a business plan, expected transaction details, source of funds and other supporting documents.
