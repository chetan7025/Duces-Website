Know Your Financial Profile

What May Banks Consider?

Each lender follows its own eligibility criteria, risk policies and assessment process. The following are common areas that may be reviewed.

  • Company establishment and operating history
  • Annual business turnover
  • Business bank account statements
  • VAT returns, where applicable
  • Business activity and industry profile
  • Existing liabilities and financial obligations
  • Credit and repayment history
  • Purpose and amount of finance requested
Meeting general criteria does not mean a company will qualify for finance. Final eligibility and approval are determined by the lender.
Document Checklist

Common Business Finance Documents

Depending on the lender and finance product, businesses may be asked to provide:

  • Valid UAE Trade Licence
  • MOA and company ownership documents
  • Shareholder and authorised signatory KYC
  • Business bank statements
  • VAT returns and tax-related records
  • Audited financial statements, if required
  • Company profile and business details
  • Existing loan and liability details
  • Finance requirement and purpose

This is a general checklist. The lender may request additional documents depending on the application.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about business finance in the UAE.

Can a newly established company apply for business finance?

Some lenders may have products for newer businesses, while others require an operating history. Eligibility depends on the lender and the finance product.

How much turnover is required for a business loan?

Turnover requirements vary between lenders and finance products. There is no single universal turnover threshold for every UAE business loan.

Are VAT returns required?

Some lenders may request VAT returns or other financial records where applicable. Requirements depend on the company's registration status and lender.

Do banks check business bank statements?

Lenders commonly review account statements to understand business activity, cash flow and financial commitments.

Does DUCES guarantee business loan approval?

No. DUCES can provide guidance and assistance within its authorised scope. All finance applications are independently assessed and decided by the lender.

Can I apply if my business already has existing liabilities?

Existing liabilities may be considered during a lender's assessment. Full and accurate disclosure is important when preparing an application.
