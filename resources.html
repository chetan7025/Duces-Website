Business Preparation Checklists

Use these lists to organise information before speaking with a service provider.

Company Setup Preparation

  • Proposed business activity
  • Preferred emirate or jurisdiction
  • Shareholder details
  • Expected visa requirement
  • Office or facility preference
  • Estimated starting budget

Bank Account Preparation

  • Trade licence and company documents
  • Shareholder identification documents
  • Business activity description
  • Expected monthly transactions
  • Source of funds information
  • Expected customer and supplier locations

Finance Preparation

  • Required finance amount
  • Purpose of finance
  • Business bank statements
  • Turnover and financial information
  • Existing loan commitments
  • Available security, if applicable
