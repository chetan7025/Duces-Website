Privacy Policy

Understand how DUCES Consultancy Services handles information shared through our website and communication channels.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how DUCES Consultancy Services ("DUCES", "we", "our" or "us") may collect, use, store and share personal information when you visit our website, submit an enquiry or communicate with our team.

Our customer-facing consultancy operations are managed through the UAE. We aim to handle personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable data protection requirements, including relevant UAE laws.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on your enquiry and the services you request, information may include:

  • First name and last name.
  • Email address and mobile number.
  • Company name, trade licence details and business information.
  • Service category and enquiry description.
  • Information voluntarily provided through email, WhatsApp, telephone or other communication channels.
  • Documents or supporting information provided for a requested service.
  • Basic technical information that may be processed by website hosting or technology providers.

Please do not submit passwords, bank login credentials, full payment card details or unnecessary sensitive identity information through public website forms.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information may be used for legitimate business and service-related purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries and consultation requests.
  • Understanding your business requirements.
  • Explaining relevant consultancy and support services.
  • Preparing quotations or discussing service requirements.
  • Coordinating a service you have requested with relevant authorised providers, where necessary.
  • Maintaining communication and service records.
  • Meeting applicable legal, regulatory and administrative obligations.
  • Improving our website and customer experience.

4. Enquiry Forms and Communication Channels

Information submitted through our website enquiry form may be used to prepare an enquiry and direct you to a communication channel such as WhatsApp.

Submitting a form or opening WhatsApp does not by itself guarantee that DUCES has received your enquiry. An enquiry is considered received when it reaches and is acknowledged through the relevant communication channel.

Telephone calls, emails and WhatsApp conversations may also involve the processing of information by the relevant communication service providers.

WhatsApp is operated by a third party and its own terms and privacy policy apply to your use of that platform. Please review those terms before sharing personal or business information.

5. UAE Operations and India Marketing Presence

DUCES provides its customer-facing consultancy and service coordination through its UAE operations.

Any India-based marketing or lead-generation support, where used, is intended to help introduce prospective customers to the UAE business. It is not presented as a separate DUCES consultancy service-delivery or payment-collection operation.

Enquiries received through our marketing channels are intended to be directed to the UAE team for follow-up. Information may be accessed or processed by authorised persons or service providers supporting these activities, subject to applicable requirements and appropriate safeguards.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell your personal information. Information may be shared where reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was provided, including:

  • With relevant banks or financial institutions when you request assistance relating to their services.
  • With authorised business setup, tax, accounting, immigration or professional service providers where required for your requested service.
  • With website hosting, communication and technology providers supporting our operations.
  • With professional advisers where necessary.
  • Where disclosure is required by applicable law, regulation or a lawful authority.

We aim to limit information shared to what is relevant and reasonably necessary for the stated purpose. Third-party providers may handle information under their own privacy and security arrangements.

7. International Data Transfers

Some communication, hosting or technology providers may process information in countries outside the UAE. Where personal information is transferred internationally, applicable legal requirements and relevant safeguards should be considered.

8. Data Security and Retention

We aim to take reasonable measures to protect personal information handled through our business processes. However, no website, electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

Actual retention periods may depend on the nature of the enquiry, the service requested and relevant legal requirements.

9. Cookies and Website Technology

Our website may use technical features required for its operation. Website hosting providers or additional tools introduced in the future may use cookies or similar technologies.

If analytics, advertising or other tracking technologies are introduced, this policy should be updated to explain their use and any choices available to visitors.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law and relevant exceptions, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, or to raise an objection or concern about its handling.

You may contact us to make a privacy-related request. We may need to verify your identity and assess the request under applicable legal requirements before responding.

11. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites, government portals, banks or other platforms. DUCES does not control the privacy practices of those third parties.

Please review the relevant third-party privacy policies before providing information through their websites or services.

12. Children's Privacy

Our website and consultancy services are intended for business owners, professionals and other individuals making business-related enquiries. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through our website.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, website features, business processes or legal requirements change.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
