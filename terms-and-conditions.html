Privacy Policy

How DUCES Consultancy Services handles information shared through its website and enquiry channels.

1. Introduction

DUCES Consultancy Services ("DUCES", "we", "our" or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you visit our website or contact us regarding our services.

We aim to handle personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable UAE data protection laws.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on your enquiry and interaction with us, information may include:

  • First name and last name.
  • Email address and mobile number.
  • Company name and business details.
  • Service category and enquiry description.
  • Information you voluntarily provide during communication.
  • Basic technical information that may be collected by website hosting or service providers.

Please do not submit passwords, bank login credentials, full payment card details or unnecessary identity documents through public website forms.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and consultation requests.
  • Understand your business requirements.
  • Explain relevant consultancy and support services.
  • Coordinate requested services with relevant authorised service providers, where necessary.
  • Maintain communication and service records.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.
  • Improve our website and customer experience.

4. Enquiry Forms and WhatsApp

Our website enquiry form may prepare your enquiry and redirect you to WhatsApp to send it to DUCES. The form currently does not itself provide a central database or guarantee that your information has been received or stored by DUCES.

When you use WhatsApp, your interaction is also subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy. Please review the privacy information provided by that platform.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell your personal information. Information may be shared where reasonably necessary:

  • With relevant service providers to address a service you have requested.
  • With authorised business setup, banking, tax, immigration or other professional providers where required for your enquiry.
  • With website hosting, communication or technology providers supporting our operations.
  • Where disclosure is required by applicable law or a lawful authority.

We aim to limit information shared to what is relevant for the stated purpose.

6. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through our business processes. However, no website, electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for its purpose or as required by applicable law. Actual retention periods may depend on the nature of the enquiry and any legal obligations.

7. Cookies and Website Technology

Our website may use basic technical features necessary for website operation. Hosting providers or future website tools may use cookies or similar technologies. If analytics, advertising or additional tracking tools are introduced, this policy should be updated to explain their use and applicable choices.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law and relevant exceptions, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, or to raise an objection or concern about its handling.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites or platforms. DUCES does not control the privacy practices of those third parties. Please review their respective privacy policies before sharing information with them.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, website features or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
