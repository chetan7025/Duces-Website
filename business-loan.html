Business Loan Eligibility

Banks and finance providers assess each application independently. The following areas may influence eligibility and facility terms.

Business Track Record

Business age, licence validity, ownership structure, operating activity and trading history.

Financial Performance

Turnover, account conduct, cash flow, profitability, financial statements and existing commitments.

Credit & Security

Credit history, repayment capacity, guarantees, collateral and other lender-specific requirements.

Important: There is no single eligibility rule applicable to every UAE bank or finance provider. Minimum turnover, business age, security, pricing and facility limits vary by lender and product.

Business Loan Document Checklist

Preparing complete and consistent information can help make the application review process more organised. Final requirements depend on the lender and facility.

Company Documents

  • Valid trade licence
  • Memorandum of Association, where applicable
  • Shareholder and ownership details
  • Office lease or Ejari, where required
  • Company profile and business activity details

Financial Documents

  • Business bank statements
  • VAT returns, where applicable
  • Financial statements or management accounts
  • Sales and purchase records
  • Existing loan and liability details
Document accuracy matters. Information supplied to banks must be genuine, consistent and supported by appropriate records.

Important Finance Considerations

Understand the responsibilities and limitations before proceeding with a finance application.

Approval & Facility Terms

Banks and finance providers independently decide eligibility, approval, facility limits, interest or profit rates, security requirements and terms. DUCES does not guarantee approval or disbursement.

Repayment Responsibility

Borrowers are responsible for understanding repayment obligations, fees, charges, security requirements and the full facility agreement before accepting an offer.

DUCES is an independent consultancy and is not a bank, lender or finance provider. Services are limited to consultancy, document preparation assistance and coordination within applicable authorised activities. All finance facilities are subject to the relevant provider's assessment and approval.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about business finance assistance and loan preparation in the UAE.

Does DUCES provide business loans directly?

No. DUCES is an independent consultancy, not a bank or lender. We provide consultancy, documentation assistance and coordination within our authorised scope.

Can a newly established company apply for a business loan?

Requirements vary by lender and facility. Some providers may require an operating history, while other products may have different eligibility conditions. The lender determines the final requirements.

What documents are normally required?

Common documents may include a trade licence, ownership documents, business bank statements, VAT records where applicable and financial statements. The final checklist depends on the lender.

Does DUCES guarantee loan approval?

No. Approval, pricing, limits, security and terms are determined solely by the relevant bank or finance provider.

Can I calculate my estimated monthly repayment?

Yes. Use the EMI calculator on this page for an indicative estimate. The lender's official repayment schedule and facility agreement determine the actual repayment.

Can DUCES assist with mortgage enquiries?

DUCES can provide consultancy and documentation assistance for mortgage enquiries, subject to applicable authorised activities and the requirements of the relevant provider.
