Business Loan Eligibility: What Lenders May Review

Every lender has its own credit policy. These are common factors that may be considered during a business finance assessment.

Business Track Record

  • Company establishment date
  • Trade licence and activity
  • Operating history
  • Customer and supplier profile

Financial Performance

  • Annual turnover
  • Bank statement activity
  • Cash flow and profitability
  • Existing loan obligations

Credit & Security

  • Credit history and repayment conduct
  • Shareholder or guarantor profile
  • Available collateral, if required
  • Debt servicing capacity
Important: A company meeting some of these indicators does not automatically qualify for finance. Each bank or finance provider applies its own credit policy, due diligence and approval process.

Business Loan Document Checklist

Prepare your company, shareholder and financial information before approaching a bank or finance provider.

Company Documents

  • Valid trade licence
  • MOA and incorporation documents, where applicable
  • Shareholder and UBO details
  • Office lease or Ejari, where applicable
  • Company profile and business activity details

Financial Documents

  • Business bank statements
  • Financial statements, if available
  • VAT returns, where applicable
  • Sales and purchase invoices
  • Existing loan and liability details

Shareholder Documents

  • Passport copies
  • Emirates ID, where applicable
  • UAE residence visa, where applicable
  • Personal financial information, if requested
  • Source of funds or wealth evidence, if requested
Facility-specific documents: Property valuation, title deed, tenancy records, purchase orders, supplier quotations, contracts, guarantees or collateral documents may also be required depending on the finance product.

Important Finance Considerations

Understand the key points before applying for a business loan or property finance facility.

Loan Approval, Pricing & Repayment Responsibilities

DUCES Consultancy Services is an independent consultancy. It is not a bank or lender and does not itself approve or disburse finance.

All finance applications are subject to the lender's independent credit assessment, KYC, compliance checks, documentation requirements and approval policy.

Interest or profit rates, fees, security requirements, guarantees, repayment periods and facility limits vary by lender and applicant profile.

Do not rely only on the advertised monthly instalment. Review the total repayment, all fees, security obligations and consequences of late or missed payments before accepting any finance offer.

Any consultancy fee payable to DUCES should be explained separately from bank charges, government fees and lender facility costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about business loans and finance assistance in the UAE.

Can a newly established UAE company apply for a business loan?

Some lenders may consider newer businesses for selected facilities, but many business finance products require an operating history and financial records. Requirements differ between lenders.

What turnover is required for a business loan?

There is no single turnover threshold that applies to every bank or finance product. Lenders may assess annual turnover, profitability, cash flow, account conduct, existing liabilities and the requested facility.

Can a Free Zone company apply for business finance?

Some lenders consider UAE Free Zone entities, subject to their own eligibility criteria, company activity, ownership structure and documentation.

Can I apply for working capital finance?

Eligible businesses may explore working capital facilities. Lenders may review business turnover, cash flow, bank statements, financial records and repayment capacity.

What is the difference between a business loan and working capital finance?

A term loan is generally structured around a defined borrowing amount and repayment schedule. Working capital facilities are intended to support short-term operating cash flow. Product terms vary by lender.

Can DUCES help with a Letter of Credit or Bank Guarantee?

DUCES can assist with understanding requirements and organising documentation for discussions with relevant banks. Issuance and approval are controlled by the bank.

Can I get a loan against my UAE property?

Some lenders offer property-backed finance to eligible applicants. Approval may depend on property valuation, ownership, existing liabilities, borrower income and lender policy.

Can DUCES guarantee loan approval?

No. DUCES provides consultancy and application preparation support. The bank or finance provider independently decides whether to approve or decline a facility.

How long does a business loan application take?

Processing time depends on the lender, facility type, completeness of documents, credit assessment and any additional information requested.

Does the EMI calculator show the final bank repayment?

No. It provides an estimate based on the values entered. The lender's official repayment schedule and facility agreement determine the actual repayment.
