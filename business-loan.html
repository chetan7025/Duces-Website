Who Can Explore Finance Options?

Eligibility varies according to the lender, product, applicant profile and supporting documents.

Established Businesses

Companies with operating history, regular business transactions and financial records.

Growing Businesses

Businesses exploring funding for expansion, inventory, equipment or operations.

Property Applicants

Eligible individuals and businesses exploring residential or commercial property finance.

Important: A new company may not qualify for every business finance product. Some lenders require an established operating history, regular account activity and financial records.

What May Lenders Evaluate?

Each lender has its own credit policy, eligibility requirements and approval process.

Business Performance

  • Business operating history
  • Annual turnover
  • Profitability and cash flow
  • Business activity and industry

Banking & Financial Records

  • Business bank statements
  • Financial statements
  • VAT returns, where applicable
  • Existing loan obligations

Applicant & Security

  • Shareholder and UBO details
  • Personal identification
  • Credit profile, where applicable
  • Collateral or guarantees, if required

Common Document Checklist

The final checklist depends on the financing product and the lender's requirements.

Business Documents

  • Valid trade licence
  • MOA and amendments, if applicable
  • Company ownership and UBO details
  • Business bank statements
  • VAT returns, where applicable

Financial Documents

  • Financial statements
  • Management accounts, if requested
  • Existing loan statements
  • Cash-flow details
  • Purpose and amount of finance required

Property Finance Documents

  • Applicant identification
  • Income and employment evidence
  • Property details and valuation, if required
  • Existing mortgage details
  • Down payment information

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about business finance and mortgage assistance in the UAE.

Can a newly established company get a business loan?

Eligibility depends on the lender and product. Many financing options require an operating history and supporting financial information.

What turnover is required for a business loan?

There is no single turnover requirement across all lenders. Minimum criteria vary by product, lender and business profile.

Can a Free Zone company apply for business finance?

Some lenders consider eligible Free Zone companies. Acceptance depends on lender policies and the company's profile.

Can I apply for working capital finance?

Eligible businesses may explore working capital products. Lenders assess business activity, cash flow, turnover and repayment capacity.

Can DUCES guarantee loan approval?

No. DUCES provides consultancy and application assistance. All financing decisions remain with the relevant lender.

Can I explore residential or commercial mortgages?

DUCES can help you understand requirements and explore relevant mortgage application routes, subject to lender eligibility and applicable permissions.

Does the EMI calculator include all loan charges?

The calculator includes the processing fee percentage entered by the user. It does not automatically include insurance, valuation charges, taxes or other lender fees.
