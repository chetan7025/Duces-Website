What Lenders May Evaluate

A strong and transparent financial profile can help lenders assess your application.

Business Performance

  • Business operating history
  • Annual turnover
  • Profitability and cash flow
  • Business activity and industry

Banking & Financial Records

  • Business bank statements
  • Financial statements
  • VAT returns, where applicable
  • Existing loan obligations

Applicant & Security

  • Shareholder and UBO details
  • Personal identification
  • Credit profile, where applicable
  • Collateral or guarantees, if required

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a newly established company get a business loan?

Eligibility depends on the lender and product. Many financing options require an operating history and supporting financial information.

What turnover is required for a business loan?

There is no single turnover requirement across all lenders. Minimum criteria vary by product, lender and business profile.

Can a Free Zone company apply for business finance?

Some lenders consider eligible Free Zone companies. Acceptance depends on the lender's policies and the company's profile.

Does DUCES guarantee loan approval?

No. DUCES provides consultancy and application assistance. All financing decisions remain with the relevant lender.

Can I apply for a mortgage through DUCES?

DUCES can help you understand requirements and explore relevant mortgage application routes, subject to lender eligibility and applicable permissions.
